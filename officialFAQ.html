Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a new garbage pile?
Go to the Home page and click the "+" button. Upload a photo, pin the location, and our team will be notified immediately.
What rewards do I get for reporting?
For every verified report, you earn "Green Points". Once you reach 500 points, you can redeem them for local store vouchers or tax rebates!
How long does it take to clean a reported spot?
Most reports are addressed within 24-48 hours. You can track the live status in the "In Progress" section.
Can I report a person for littering?
Yes. Use the "Reported" section. If you have video or photo evidence of the violator, they may be fined based on city laws.
