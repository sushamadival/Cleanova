About Our Mission

Empowering citizens to build a cleaner, greener, and more accountable city.

1. Report

Citizens capture and upload photos of waste mismanagement in real-time.

2. Earn

Verified reports earn Discounts on utility bills.

3. Enforce

Identified violators are issued Fines to ensure accountability.

Our Impact Pillars

Clean City: Active reduction of street-side pollution.
Verified: Official government verification for every claim.
Transparent: Fair and automated penalty systems.
Rewards: Turning civic duty into financial gains.
